Storage and transport, handled

If tomatoes cannot wait on the farm, KUZA arranges temporary cold storage. Buyers can have transport coordinated for them, or collect the produce themselves.

  • Temporary storage when a harvest is ready before delivery
  • Transport coordinated by KUZA, priced separately from the produce
  • A clear delivery timeline for farmer, buyer and KUZA
  • Quantities and handovers recorded at every stage

For buyers

Restaurants, hotels, retailers and traders get a reliable supply they can plan around.

  • See upcoming harvests by location, quality and date
  • Reserve the quantity you need with a deposit
  • Track your order from confirmation to delivery
  • Confirm receipt to close the order
